### What these are

**photosvi** — finds the dopamine transients in a photometry trace and measures what a drug did to
their rate, both out of one model instead of a threshold.

**the heart** — a microcontroller that generates a real ECG live, from a fuzzy model of the heart's
electrical activity.

**Cove** — you build your own map of concepts and fly around it as a star chart; you are the
linguist, it is the scaffolding.
